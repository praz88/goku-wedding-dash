# Sound credits

All files here are baked by `tools/make-audio.py` from these sources, then exported as MP3.

**Field recordings (CC0 1.0, public domain)** — by **Joseph Sardin**, published on
[BigSoundBank](https://bigsoundbank.com), collected by the [NoiseSmith](https://github.com/zimba7768/noisesmith) project:
- `rain_loop` ← "Summer Rain on Terrace" (s1019)
- `wind_loop` ← "Whistling of the Wind #2" (s0155) + "Wind from inside #1" (s1714)
- `thunder_1..3`, and the reversed swells inside `sting_*` and `riser` ← "Thunder" #2, #6, #1, #8 (s3113, s3179, s2718, s3181)

**Creak recordings (CC0 1.0)** via OpenGameArt, collected by NoiseSmith:
- `creak_door` ← "Creaky Door Hinge Spooky" by wforster1gmailcom
- `creak_vault` ← `door_creak_open.wav` from "Various Sound Effects" by laleksic (pitched down, with a synthesized metal hit)
- `creak_floor_1..3` ← `floor_creak1..3.wav` from "Various Sound Effects" by laleksic

**Synthesized in the script (CC0):** drone bed, music bed (pads + a flat music box), stings, whispers,
breath, heartbeat, footsteps, the Manager's chuckle, phone rings, slam, shutter, drip, horn.
